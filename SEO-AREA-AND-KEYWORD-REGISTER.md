Prepared 29 September 2026. Use with MAANE-OOTA-MASTER-PLAN.md.

## How to use this register

These are **120 proposed local-area page candidates**, spanning six practical acquisition groups. This is a planning shortlist, not verified delivery coverage, not an exhaustive list of Bengaluru, and not a researched keyword-volume report. Outer catchments include places beyond a strict city boundary; operations must choose the commercial service boundary before activation. Grouping is for work allocation, not official municipal geography.

Every row starts as `draft`; geography verification, PIN mapping, actual kitchens, capacity, delivery and local content are required before publication. Do not give every page the same kitchen list and replace the place name. Merge geographically or commercially overlapping candidates unless each has a useful independent purpose.

For every area row below:

- Full route = `/home-food-delivery/bengaluru/` + the listed slug.
- Primary keyword = `homemade food delivery in ` + the listed area name.
- Secondary terms = `home food in {area}`, `home cooked meals in {area}`, `home-style food in {area}` and `food like home in {area}`, used naturally when accurate.
- Title = `Homemade Food Delivery in {area} | MAANE OOTA`.
- H1 = `Homemade Food Delivery in {area}`.
- Canonical = approved host + full route; sitemap inclusion is false until the publication gate passes.
- Initial status = draft; PINs, coordinates, partner count, prices and keyword volumes remain unverified/null.

This supplies a deterministic URL, keyword, title and H1 for every candidate without fabricating service facts. Metadata and content must still be checked for readability and truth before release. Full visible content requirements are in section 7 of the master plan.

## 7. Geography verification notes

Validate ambiguous names such as Dasarahalli, Kodigehalli, Bagalur and Channasandra against city context and coordinates before assigning a slug. Channasandra here is an East/Whitefield-catchment candidate; verify that intent rather than accidentally connecting another locality with the same name. Likewise resolve Ullal in the Bengaluru context.

Review possible overlap between Indiranagar/HAL 2nd Stage, Kengeri/Kengeri Satellite Town, Yelahanka/Yelahanka New Town and nearby layouts. Parent-child candidates do not automatically require separate indexable pages. Do not claim outer settlements are within the municipal boundary simply because they are part of the business catchment.

Alias examples to verify: Malleswaram/Malleshwaram, Yeshwantpur/Yeshwanthpur, Rajarajeshwari Nagar/RR Nagar, Ulsoor/Halasuru, Frazer Town/Pulakeshinagar, Electronics City/Electronic City. Aliases help search resolve the same entity; they do not create duplicate pages.

Future blocks/phases/streets: add only after evidence of distinct demand and actual service information. Examples include a verified JP Nagar phase or Electronic City phase. Landmark searches such as “near Manyata Tech Park” can first resolve to existing kitchens/catchments without another indexable page. No PIN, landmark or street pages are required merely to reach a page-count target.

## 11. SEO publication worksheet

For each candidate, the CMS must record: location ID/name/aliases; verified geographic source; canonical URL; primary intent; title/description/H1; actual kitchens; active items/plans; slot coverage; delivery notes; price source; photo rights; local FAQ answers; nearby links; overlap review; content owner; review time; publish/noindex state; sitemap state; last material update; next review date.

Content preview must distinguish placeholder fields from publishable facts. Clicking Publish runs the gate and returns missing requirements in plain language. Record manual overrides with evidence and actor; an override must not permit false service claims.

## 12. Measurement and expansion rule

In the first month, establish baselines for search impressions, qualified visits, address checks, serviceable sessions, delivered orders and contribution per locality. Use a rolling period with sufficient sample size; do not shut down a newly indexed page just because it has not ranked in a week.

Every week, decide: improve existing page, improve supplier availability, add new distinct intent, merge duplicate page, or leave unchanged. A location with search traffic but no delivered orders needs an availability/conversion investigation, not automatically more blog posts.

Targets such as 100 published pages and all six territories represented are operational milestones. Rankings, indexing, traffic and orders cannot be guaranteed. The aim is 120 useful local entry points as supply matures, with room to add additional verified localities.
